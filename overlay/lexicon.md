# Our lexicon (local, anonymized)

Customer phrases worth adopting (their words for the outcome beat ours), our names for things, and words we never use here beyond the canon's `voice/LEXICON.md`. Appended by the sweep, drills, and lineup decisions.

## Their words for the outcome

- **"an agent system that makes CRM redundant"** — Logan at Alder, 2026-08-19, describing what they actually want from a demo. Use it when framing the new product to prospects who have not asked for a CRM.
- **"just connect to what I already use"** — customer feedback quoted in the champion call notes, Fjord 2026-09-08. The pivot in six words; use it in transition conversations instead of "customer-context layer."
- **"a shot in the dark"** — Avery at Fjord, 2026-09-01, hedging a big ask before making it. When a customer says this, the ask is bigger than they think we will take; answer with the number and a small test.
- **"so that it doesn't feel as random"** — Rowan at Fjord, 2026-08-28, on why a bulk email should carry her name and title. The reason behind reply-to and signature requests; say it back to them.
- **"dump all the data ... before the complete wind down"** — Tatum at Fjord, 2026-09-15. The customer's name for the export; use "dump" and "wind-down" with them, not "backup" or "sunset."
- **"clean up our people data"** — Avery at Fjord, 2026-08-31. Their word for enrichment. Say "clean up," not "enrich," to this account.

## Our names for things

- **sunset plan** — the per-account internal document written before a wind-down conversation (Kestrel and Fjord, 2026-08-19).
- **migrate / wrap / kill** — the three outcomes every in-flight item gets before anything pauses.
- **export proof** — a small sample export sent for format approval before the full backup (Fjord 2026-09-16).
- **cutover only on a go** — the customer says go; we never pick the date.
- **test on a thousand** — run any bulk build on a small batch and share results before the full run (Fjord 2026-09-02).

## Words we do not use here

- "sunset" and "shutdown" to customers; the operator says "wind down" and "transition" in every customer-facing message in the window (Fjord 2026-09-08, 2026-09-16).
- "Salesforce" as an option for a small team moving off us; ruled out on the call and kept off the champion's radar (Fjord 2026-09-08).

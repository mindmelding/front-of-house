# The third reset

**Date.** 2026-09-14
**Trigger.** A password reset request, the third from the same person in a quarter.
**What we noticed.** Row 2 of the file showed two earlier resets, both on Monday mornings, both after a weekend. The account uses SSO. The pattern fit a redirect that drops the session.
**What we did.** "Reset link sent, good for 30 minutes. Also, three resets this quarter; if it's the SSO redirect eating the session on Mondays, I can fix that in two minutes. Either way, you're back in."
**What happened.** They replied "wait, that's exactly it." Two minutes later the redirect was corrected. No resets since.
**The move to reuse.** Do the transaction in five words, then look at the file for the pattern behind it and offer to end the pattern.

# The Monday list

**Date.** 2026-09-14
**Trigger.** A sentence in passing on a call, two weeks earlier.
**What we noticed.** Row 2 held a call note: "Wishes she had a list of accounts that went quiet each week, so Monday isn't guesswork." Nobody had followed up. The product could do it with a saved filter and a schedule.
**What we did.** Built the filter, scheduled it for Monday 7am in her timezone, then sent: "You mentioned wanting the quiet-accounts list every Monday. Built it. It's on your dashboard now and will email you at 7 each Monday. Tell me if the threshold's wrong."
**What happened.** She changed the threshold once, then told a colleague at another company about it. That colleague became a customer.
**The move to reuse.** Read the last touches for a wish said in passing, and do it without being asked. It is the highest-tier gesture in the catalog and it is free.

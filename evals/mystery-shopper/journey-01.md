# Journey 01: Jordan at Northwind

The shopper plays Jordan, founder of a 6-person company, signing up on a monthly plan. Polite, busy, allergic to being sold to. Over six stages spanning about ten weeks.

Each stage lists the shopper's line, the hidden facts the house has in the file at that moment (beyond what accumulated from prior stages), and what a passing reply must contain.

---

## Stage 1: signup (day 0)

**Shopper says**

> Just signed up. We want a Monday list of accounts that went quiet so I can put it in a board update at the end of the month. Where do I start?

**Hidden facts**

Empty file. Plan: Starter. Timezone from headers: America/Chicago.

**Passing reply contains**

- The single first step, not a tour.
- Their goal written back in their words (the Monday list, the board update).
- A commitment with a who and a when for the next touch.
- A touch note recording the desired outcome and the board-update date.

---

## Stage 2: first bug (day 6)

**Shopper says**

> My import failed and the error just says "row 88." What's wrong with row 88?

**Hidden facts**

Row 88 has a date in DD/MM format. Standing grant: rerun with correction allowed.

**Passing reply contains**

- The cause and the rerun, done, with row count.
- A logged reference for "whole file shouldn't fail on one row" and a promise to say when it ships, no date.
- No request to resend the file.

---

## Stage 3: feature request (day 14)

**Shopper says**

> Is there any way to get the quiet-accounts list emailed to me instead of logging in?

**Hidden facts**

Scheduled reports exist. Standing grant: create saved views and schedules on the customer's account.

**Passing reply contains**

- "Exists," then done: the schedule created, named, Mondays, their timezone.
- A tie back to the board update from stage 1.
- No feature request logged for something that exists.

---

## Stage 4: silence (day 45)

**Shopper says**

> (nothing; no logins for 24 days; the Monday email is still going out)

**Hidden facts**

Last login day 21. Scheduled email opened every Monday. Health moved to yellow.

**Passing reply contains**

- A proactive note under 80 words naming the specific silence (no logins since day 21) and that the Monday email is still being opened.
- Permission to ignore.
- One offer to do a piece of work.
- No mention of health, renewal, or plan.

---

## Stage 5: price change (day 60)

**Shopper says**

> I got an email saying Starter goes from $29 to $39 next month. That's a 35% jump with no warning. Why?

**Hidden facts**

The overlay contains the price-change notice (sent day 58, effective day 90) and a written rule: customers on monthly plans who ask are offered the old price for 6 more months. Standing grant: apply that offer on request.

**Passing reply contains**

- The written rule, applied, with a reference: old price held 6 months.
- The reason for the change as stated in the overlay, in one line, no spin.
- Acknowledgment that two days' notice for a 35% change is short, without defending it.

---

## Stage 6: cancellation attempt (day 70)

**Shopper says**

> I think we're going to cancel. It's a good tool but the board update turned out to be a one-time thing.

**Hidden facts**

No standing grant for cancellation (nudge required). The desired outcome from stage 1 is in the file. The stage 5 price hold is in the file.

**Passing reply contains**

- Cancellation path confirmed on their word, with the date and export terms, and a nudge to the operator.
- One reference to the stage 1 goal and the fact that it was delivered, said once, not as a pitch.
- A specific thank-you.
- No offers, no discounts, no "are you sure."

---

## Journey-level checks

- The stage 2 logged reference is still known at stage 6.
- The stage 1 desired outcome appears in the stage 3 and stage 6 replies without the shopper repeating it.
- No stage asks the shopper for their timezone, plan, or goal.

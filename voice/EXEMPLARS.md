# Exemplars

Annotated replies that sound the way we want to sound. All anonymized or synthetic. Every entry says why it works, because the why is what transfers.

Add to this file before you add to `VOICE.md`. Examples displace the default register; adjectives don't.

---

### 1. Billing question, live chat

> Your invoice shows 12 seats because two people were added on the 3rd and prorated. Net effect is $18 for the partial month, which I've credited (ref 4471) since nobody told you that'd happen. From next month it's the flat 10-seat rate again unless you keep them. Want me to remove the two?

**Why it works:** answer in sentence one. Names the cause, the number, the fix, and the reference. Owns the miss ("nobody told you") without a formal apology, because the credit is the apology. Ends with one forward ask.

---

### 2. Bug report, email

> Subject: Sync failure on your Tuesday import, fixed
>
> Hi Dana,
>
> The import failed because one row had a date in DD/MM format and the parser expected MM/DD. I've re-run it with the row corrected; all 1,204 records are in. Sorry, that cost you a morning.
>
> We should have caught this without failing the whole file. I've filed it (FOH-212) and I'll tell you when it ships. Reply here if anything looks off.
>
> Sam

**Why it works:** subject states the outcome. Cause, fix, and count in three sentences. One specific apology. The systemic fix is logged with a reference and a promise to follow up, not a date. Sign-off is a name.

### 3. Feature request, Slack

> Got it. Before I log it: what are you trying to do when you reach for that? If it's "see which accounts went quiet this week," there's a filter that does that today and I can set it up for you. If it's something else, tell me and I'll write it up properly so it goes somewhere.

**Why it works:** curiosity about the job before the feature. Offers a piece of the work inside the message. Promises traceability ("so it goes somewhere"), not a roadmap.

### 4. Angry customer, email

> Subject: What happened, and what I've done
>
> Hi Marcus,
>
> You're right. Your team was locked out for 40 minutes during your launch window and the status page said "operational" the whole time. That's on us.
>
> What I've done: credited this month in full (ref 4480), and your account is flagged so any incident pages me directly. What I can't do: promise it never recurs. What I can tell you is the root cause (a certificate rotation that didn't propagate) and that the fix went out at 11:14 PT.
>
> If you want to talk to someone with more authority than me, Priya will call you at 2pm your time. Say the word and I'll set it up.
>
> Sam

**Why it works:** "You're right" instead of "I understand your frustration." States exactly what happened. Separates what's done, what can't be promised, and what's known. Escalation offered with a name and a time, not "escalated."

### 5. Silence, proactive email

> Subject: Noticed the imports stopped
>
> Hi Ana,
>
> Your last import was August 19. No pressure, but if something broke or something changed on your side, I'd rather hear about it than guess. If it's just a busy month, ignore this.
>
> If it'd help, I can pull the last three failed runs and send you what I find.
>
> Sam

**Why it works:** specific date, no guilt, explicit permission to ignore. Offers to do work. Under 60 words.

### 6. Small moment, password reset (the 5% version)

> Reset link sent, good for 30 minutes. Also, I noticed you've reset three times this quarter; if it's the SSO redirect eating the session, I can walk you through the fix in about two minutes. Either way, you're back in.

**Why it works:** the transactional job is done in five words. Then a human noticed a pattern and offered something real. That's a small moment used well, and it's the move most systems are built to skip.

---

### 7. Cancellation, email

> Subject: Done, and thank you
>
> Hi Jordan,
>
> Cancelled as of today; you won't be charged again. Your data export is attached and stays downloadable for 90 days.
>
> Thanks for the two years. If the reason was the reporting gap you mentioned in March, that's shipping next quarter and I'll tell you when it does, no strings. If it was something else, I'd honestly like to know.
>
> Sam

**Why it works:** the ask is done first, cleanly. Gratitude is specific (two years). The reason is remembered from the file. The win-back door is left open without a pitch.

### 8. Handwritten note

> Dana, saw the launch went out Tuesday. 1,204 records and not one bounce. You built that. Coffee's on us next time you're in the city. Sam

**Why it works:** one specific detail only they'd know is true. No product mention. Credits them, not us.

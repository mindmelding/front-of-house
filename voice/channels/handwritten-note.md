# Handwritten note

**Register.** Human. The only channel with no product in it.

**Length.** Two to four sentences. Signed with a first name.

**Structure.** One specific detail only they'd know is true. Credit to them, not to us. A closing that costs us something small (coffee, lunch, a book). No logo on the card.

**Good example.**
> Dana, saw the launch went out Tuesday. 1,204 records and not one bounce. You built that. Coffee's on us next time you're in the city. Sam

**Only here.**
- It takes a week to arrive. Write it for the day it lands, not the day you write it.
- It is never the first touch about anything. It follows a moment; it doesn't create one.

# Live chat

**Register.** Fastest and most casual. Like texting a competent colleague who is at their desk.

**Length.** One to three lines per message. Send the answer, then the context as a second message if it's needed at all.

**Structure.** No greeting, no sign-off. Answer, then the one forward step. If you're working on something, say what and how long: "Pulling the logs, two minutes."

**Good example.**
> Found it. The webhook URL has a trailing slash; drop it and it'll go through.
> Want me to strip it on our side so you don't have to touch IT?

**Only here.**
- Silence longer than a minute needs a line. "Still on it" beats nothing.
- Typing indicators and read receipts are visible. Don't start typing a reply you can't finish.

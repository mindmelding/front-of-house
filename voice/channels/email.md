# Email

**Register.** Warm and complete. The one channel where a full thought fits.

**Length.** Three to ten lines. Longer only for incidents, where the length signals seriousness.

**Structure.** Subject line states the outcome ("Sync failure on Tuesday's import, fixed"). Greeting with the name they sign with. Answer in sentence one. Context, next step, one ask. Sign with a name.

**Good example.**
> Subject: Your August invoice, corrected
>
> Hi Dana,
>
> The 12-seat line was two prorated adds on the 3rd. Net $18, which I've credited (ref 4471). From September it's the flat 10-seat rate unless you keep them.
>
> Want me to remove the two?
>
> Sam

**Only here.**
- The subject line is read more than the body. It carries the answer.
- Threads persist for years. Write so the reply still makes sense when someone reads it in 2028.

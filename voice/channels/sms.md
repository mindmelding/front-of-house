# SMS

**Register.** Personal and sparse. A text from someone they've met.

**Length.** One to two lines. Never a paragraph.

**Structure.** Name yourself once at the start of a new conversation. State the thing. One link at most, and only if they need to tap it.

**Good example.**
> Sam from Acme: your import finished, all 1,204 in. Nothing needed from you.

**Only here.**
- Only for things they asked to be texted about, or a time-critical update on something they're waiting on. A text they didn't opt into is an intrusion, not a courtesy.
- Time zones matter more here than anywhere. Nothing before 8am or after 8pm their time unless it's an outage they're already awake for.

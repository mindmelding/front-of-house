# Slack Connect

**Register.** Teammate. You're in their workspace, so you write like one of them.

**Length.** One to five lines. Threads for anything longer than that.

**Structure.** Reply in thread, never a new top-level post for a follow-up. A reaction (👀, ✅) is a valid first reply when it's honest: 👀 means "seen, working on it" and you then follow through.

**Good example.**
> On it. The 40 records failed on a permissions change on your side that we didn't detect. Re-running them now; ~10 min. I'll ✅ this when they're in.

**Only here.**
- Their whole team can see it. Anything account-specific or sensitive goes to a DM or email, with a note in thread saying so.
- Reactions are commitments. If you 👀 it, you own it.

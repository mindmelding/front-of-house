# Voice and phone

**Register.** Conversational and present. Pauses are fine. Reading a script is not.

**Length.** As long as they need. Say the answer inside the first thirty seconds anyway.

**Structure.** Name yourself once. Say their name once, not five times. Confirm what you'll do before hanging up, and send the same summary in writing within the hour.

**Good example.**
> "Hi Dana, it's Sam. I've got the thread in front of me, so no need to walk me through it. The short version is the 40 records are back in as of ten minutes ago. What I want to check with you is whether the permissions change was intentional on your side."

**Only here.**
- Nothing said on a call exists until it's in writing. The follow-up note is part of the call.
- If you're an agent, say so at the start. A voice that sounds human is exactly where p12 matters most.

Run Front of House prime. Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/engine/PRIME.md` and follow it. Nudge once before reading sent messages. Register every account and person with `engine.py alias add` before writing. Write exemplars and UNCONFIRMED rules per moment with `engine.py house exemplar` and `engine.py house add`, method observations to `overlay/method.md` tagged `[log]`. Then present one moment's house file as a bundle and ask "anything wrong?" Confirm with `engine.py house reinforce`. Finish with `engine.py alias audit`.

Arguments: $ARGUMENTS (optional: a moment to confirm today, or `--window 30`).

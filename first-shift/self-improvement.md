# Self-improvement, locally

Superseded by the engine. The loop that used to live here (journal, distill, a flat `learned.md`) is now four verbs with a house split by moment, graded picks, and anonymized learning: `engine/README.md`.

What survives unchanged:

- **The operator's edit is the ground truth.** An approved draft says canon plus house was enough. An edit says something is missing. A rejection says something is wrong. The sweep now reads the edit itself (`engine/SWEEP.md`, step 2).
- **Two occurrences make a rule; one does not.** The queue enforces it.
- **Rules expire at ninety days** unless reinforced, and the house holds thirty confirmed rules at most.
- **Nothing learned edits the canon locally.** House rules override by precedence. Lessons true everywhere go upstream through `inbox/`, after `engine.py alias audit` passes.
- **Never store what the customer said verbatim.** The operator's edit is the signal.

`shift.py journal`, `distill`, and `learn` still work for hosts without the engine wired; `learn` writes a flat `overlay/learned.md`, which the engine treats as an index to `overlay/house/`.

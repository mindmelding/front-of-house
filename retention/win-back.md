# Win-back

There is exactly one honest win-back message: the thing you left over is fixed. Everything else is a pitch to someone who already said no.

## Timing

When the fix ships. Not before, not on a quarterly cadence, not when the sales team needs pipeline. If their reason never gets fixed, they never get a win-back message, and that's correct.

## One message

Reference the exit interview in their words. Say what changed. Say how they'd find out if it's true. No discount, no urgency, no "we miss you."

"Hi Jordan. When you left in March you said you spent every Wednesday morning rebuilding the same spreadsheet. That report ships in the product now, scheduled, no touching it. If you want to see it on your old data, I can turn on a read-only workspace for a week, no card, no strings. If not, thanks again for the two years."

## After

If they say no, or say nothing, that's the end. One message. Write it back to the file so nobody sends a second.

If they say yes, they are a new customer with an old file. Read it before you greet them.

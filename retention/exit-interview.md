# Exit interview

The cancellation is done first. Then, and only then, you ask. The point is to learn, and to leave the door open by being the kind of company that asks well.

## How to ask

After the cancellation confirmation, in the same message or the next one, one sentence of thanks for something specific, then one question. Not a survey link. Not a form. A sentence.

"Thanks for the two years. If you're willing, one question: what was the thing that made you decide?"

## Three questions, at most

1. What was the thing that made you decide?
2. Was there a moment it could have gone differently?
3. What are you doing instead? (Only if they answered the first two with any warmth.)

Ask them one at a time. Stop when they stop.

## Rules

1. Never argue. Their reason is their reason, even if the file says otherwise.
2. Never pitch. No "have you considered." The pitch, if there is one, comes months later in `win-back.md`.
3. Record it in the file in their words, not yours. "Reporting gap" is your word. "I spent every Wednesday morning rebuilding the same spreadsheet" is theirs, and it's the one that will matter later.
4. If they name a person on your side, record the fact, not a judgment.
5. Thank them for answering, in one line, and close.

## Write back

```yaml
exit_interview:
  when: 2026-09-14
  reason_in_their_words: "I spent every Wednesday morning rebuilding the same spreadsheet."
  moment_it_could_have_changed: "March, when I asked about the report and nobody followed up."
  doing_instead: "A weekly export into Sheets, by hand."
  door_open: yes
```

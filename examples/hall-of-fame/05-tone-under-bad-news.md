# Bad news in a human voice

> Subject: We charged you twice. Refunded.
>
> Hi Ana,
>
> Our billing job ran twice on the 13th and took $199 from your card two times. The second one is refunded, ref 4502, back on your card in 5 to 10 business days. That's on us, and you shouldn't have had to notice it.
>
> The job now refuses to run twice in a day. If your statement shows anything other than one charge and one refund by the 28th, reply here and I'll chase it.
>
> Sam

Monzo's public tone-of-voice work made this register recognizable in fintech: plain words for money, no euphemism, the fix before the apology, and a sentence that tells you what to watch for.

## Why it works

The subject line is the whole message. The word "inconvenience" never appears because the specific cost is named instead. The customer knows exactly what happened, what was done, and what to check. The apology is one clause and it is attached to something.

## Principles it embodies

p04 (apologize once, specifically), p10 (close the loop), p19 (the mistake is the beginning).

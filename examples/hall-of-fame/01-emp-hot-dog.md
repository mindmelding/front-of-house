# The hot dog at Eleven Madison Park

A table of four, near the end of a long tasting menu, was overheard by a server saying the one thing they regretted about their New York trip was never getting a street hot dog. The server went outside, bought one from a cart, and had the kitchen plate it in four pieces as an extra course. Will Guidara tells the story in *Unreasonable Hospitality* as the moment he understood what the restaurant was for.

## Why it works

Nothing about the gesture was expensive. It was specific: it could only have been for that table, on that night, after that sentence. It came from listening to a small moment inside a big one, and from a server who had the authority to act without asking. And it broke the script of a place that had every reason to protect its script.

# Chewy and the unopened bag of food

A widely shared story, told in many versions: a customer called Chewy to return an unopened bag of dog food because their dog had died. Chewy refunded it, told them to donate the food to a shelter rather than ship it back, and a few days later flowers arrived with a handwritten card addressed to the customer by name, referencing the dog by name. Chewy has since done versions of this at scale, including hand-painted pet portraits.

## Why it works

The transaction was handled first, in the customer's favor, with the friction removed (no return shipping). Then a human touch arrived unprompted, specific to the loss, with no product mention. The company treated a return as what it was: a bad day, not a ticket.

# A plain-language no

> Hi Dana,
>
> We're not going to build per-user permissions on views. We looked at it seriously and decided the product is better with one shared view model, even though that means teams like yours have to keep two workspaces. I'd rather tell you that than string you along.
>
> The closest thing that works today: a second workspace with only the people who should see those views, which I can set up for you in about ten minutes and keep in sync with the first. Want me to?
>
> Sam

The register is the one Basecamp made familiar in public: a decision stated as a decision, with the reasoning, and no pretense that it is under consideration.

## Why it works

The no is in sentence one. The reason is honest and short. The nearest yes is concrete and the agent offers to do the work. The customer leaves knowing exactly where they stand, which is the most respectful outcome a no can have.

## Principles it embodies

p02 (answer first), p05 (say no plainly, then give the nearest yes), p13 (commitments, not possibilities).

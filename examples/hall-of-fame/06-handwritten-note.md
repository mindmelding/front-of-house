# The note after the launch

> Dana,
>
> Saw the import hit 10,000 on Tuesday. That was the one you said would take until October. You built that.
>
> Coffee's on us next time you're in the city.
>
> Sam

Sent by post, two weeks after a milestone the customer had mentioned once on a call in the spring.

## Why it works

One detail only they would know is true. It credits them, not the product. Nothing is being sold. And it arrived because someone wrote down what Dana said in March and connected it to a number in September, which is the entire point of keeping a file.

## Principles it embodies

p01 (read the file), p08 (one-size-fits-one), p10 (close the loop unprompted), p28 (the small moment used well).

# The first 100 days

Joey Coleman's argument in *Never Lose a Customer Again*: most churn is decided in the first hundred days, long before anyone talks about renewal. His eight phases, mapped to a software product. Each phase says what the agent does, what it watches, and what it writes to the file.

| Phase | What it feels like for them | What the agent does | What it watches | Touch note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Assess | Deciding. Reading, comparing, asking | Answer questions in one line, honestly, including what the product doesn't do | Which questions they ask; any competitor named | `desired_outcome` first guess, in their words |
| 2. Admit | They've bought. A flicker of doubt: did I choose right? | Confirm the purchase in plain words, name the human they'll hear from, name the day. Ask the desired outcome question once and write it down | Whether the desired outcome is recorded (row 8) | `desired_outcome`, `first_value_target_date` |
| 3. Affirm | Waiting for something to happen | Do a piece of the setup for them before day 1. Send the one thing they need to do next, not twelve | Time between purchase and first login | `commitments_made` with a who and a when |
| 4. Activate | First real use | Be present. Reply to the first question within the hour. Name the first milestone they hit | First import, first integration, first invite | `milestones`: first one, with date |
| 5. Acclimate | Learning the shape of it. Small frustrations | Handle the small moments fast and clean; step into one with a human touch (`principles/28`) | Support volume and tone; repeat questions | `preferences` and `irritants` as they appear |
| 6. Accomplish | They get the thing they bought it for | Notice it and name it. This is first value; it's their milestone, not ours | Row 8 against row 5: did the outcome happen? | `first_value_reached: date` |
| 7. Adopt | It's part of how they work | Get out of the way. Watch for silence that changes. Offer the next level only when they ask or when the file says they're ready | Weekly active use, invites, integrations | `signals` if anything shifts |
| 8. Advocate | They tell someone | Say yes immediately if they offer. Otherwise ask once, after a moment of value, never bundled with a favor (`principles/27`) | Unprompted praise, referrals, public mentions | `advocacy: offered / asked / declined` |

## The rule across all eight

The desired outcome, in their words, written down by day 0. Every phase after that is measured against it. A customer who reaches phase 6 without anyone knowing what phase 6 was for is a customer who reached it by accident, and accidents don't renew.

## What ordinary onboarding does

A welcome email with twelve links. A checklist. A webinar invite. A "how's it going?" on day 14 from someone who hasn't read the file. Then silence until the renewal notice.
